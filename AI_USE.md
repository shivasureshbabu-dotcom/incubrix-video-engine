\# AI Use Disclosure



\## Purpose

AI tools were used as development assistance while building the IncuBrix Script and Voiceover to B-roll Video Engine.



\## How AI Was Used

\- Helped explain project requirements and break development into manageable tasks.

\- Assisted with drafting and reviewing Python code, tests, and documentation.

\- Helped troubleshoot development environment and command-line errors.

\- Assisted with explanations of audio analysis, scene timing, footage selection, video rendering, captions, and Git.



\## Human Review and Testing

Generated suggestions were reviewed and integrated during development. The project was tested locally, including automated tests and final video validation. Passing tests do not guarantee that every requirement or hidden evaluation case is satisfied.



\## Limitations

Some capabilities use simplified heuristics. Audio loudness is used as a proxy for emphasis, caption timings may be approximate, and footage relevance and licensing require further verification. These limitations are documented in the project report.



\## Responsibility

The developer is responsible for reviewing the implementation, checking third-party licenses, protecting API credentials, and validating the final submission.
